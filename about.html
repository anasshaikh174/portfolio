BACKGROUND & PERSPECTIVE

About Anas Shaikh

Undergraduate computer science engineering student exploring the intersection of clean software architecture and intuitive frontend user interfaces.

I am currently in my third year of pursuing a B.Tech in Computer Science (CMPN A). Over the past three years of coursework and independent projects, I found my core interest in frontend web development: writing clear semantic markup, orchestrating responsive CSS systems, and creating web applications that feel fast and purposeful.

Rather than jumping between disparate framework trends without depth, my focus is on mastering foundational web technologies — semantic HTML5, modern CSS specifications (Flexbox, Grid, custom properties), and vanilla JavaScript. Having solid fundamentals makes understanding tools like React intuitive and grounded.

Beyond solo coding, I actively participate in hackathons. Qualifying for Round 2 in the Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026 and competing in SIH 2025 taught me how to analyze problem statements objectively, coordinate across a team under time pressure, and design solutions that address real-world workflows.
